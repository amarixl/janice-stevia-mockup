Our Materials

Every Janice Stevia Design creation is made from recycled water-based vegan leather: 58% recycled polyester, 37% recycled water-based polyurethane, and 5% kaolin. No solvents, no harmful chemicals, no animal-derived materials.

Recycled water-based polyurethane (PU) represents a significant evolution from traditional solvent-based PU. Its chemical footprint is smaller, its feel is supple, and its resistance to deformation lets it keep its structure over time — even under intensive daily use.

Durability testing was carried out in-house: pressed and stressed repeatedly to simulate daily use, the material returns to its original shape without leaving any marks or visible creases. It combines lightness, flexibility, and durability.

It’s a choice rooted in conviction: to design pieces that last, that don’t compromise animal welfare, and that respect resources. Recycled water-based vegan leather is, today, the most rigorous alternative available for premium leather goods. To learn more, visit our Our Quality page.

Yes. The Janice Stevia brand holds PETA – Approved Vegan certification, confirming the complete absence of animal-derived materials in our collections.

We estimate the lifespan of our creations at approximately five years of daily use — a longevity we attribute to the quality of the material, the strength of the assembly, and the rigour of our quality control at every stage of production.

To preserve the beauty and durability of your Janice Stevia Design bag, clean it gently with a soft, slightly damp cloth. For stains, use a mild soap diluted in lukewarm water, then wipe with a clean cloth and let it air dry naturally.

Avoid harsh products such as bleach, alcohol, acetone, or any other solvent, as well as abrasive brushes and products meant for animal leather. For optimal storage, keep your bag in the protective pouch included with your order, in a dry place away from humidity and direct sunlight.

When not in use, fill it with tissue paper or a soft cloth to help it hold its shape, and avoid compressing it under heavy objects. Regular care will help preserve its elegance and extend its lifespan.

The recycled PU leather used in our creations offers good resistance to water and everyday splashes.

However, avoid placing it near a direct heat source, such as a radiator or hair dryer, as this could alter the material.

To preserve the appearance and durability of your bag, it’s best to avoid prolonged exposure to heavy rain or excessive humidity.

Warranty

Bags and Accessories

All bags and accessories are covered by an 18-month warranty from the date of purchase, upon presentation of valid proof of purchase.

The warranty covers:

  • Any manufacturing defect affecting the quality or proper function of the product
  • Zipper issues
  • Stitching defects
  • Component assembly defects

The warranty does not cover:

  • Normal wear and tear from use
  • Snags, cuts, or tears
  • Scratches or marks on hardware
  • Colour transfer or dye bleeding
  • Product deformation
  • Damage caused by improper care or product modifications
  • Damage resulting from misuse, accidents, or negligence

This warranty applies only to manufacturing defects and does not cover damage attributable to normal use or external causes.
